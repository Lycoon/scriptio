# Features

- **Cloud Synchronization**
- **Real-time Collaboration**
- **Cross-platform** (Windows, MacOS, browser)
- **Offline-first** (no account needed)
- **Industry Formatting** (customizable layout, page breaks, dual dialogue)
- **Compatibility Formats** (PDF, Fountain, Final Draft)
- **Production Ready** (revisions, scene & page locking)
- **Scene Management** (easy navigation, reordering)
- **Character Management** (color highlighting, synopsis)
- **Beat Board** (story cards, outlining, voice notes)
- **Document Hierarchy** (folders, drafts, boards)
- **Smart Editor** (context aware auto-completion, spellchecker)
- **Customization** (themes & custom keybinds)
- **Statistics** (dialogue distribution, character frequency)
- **Read Aloud** (based on TTS AI voice models)
- **The Shelve** (store dialogue or action alternates)
- **Search & Replace** (advanced filtering)
- **Script Comments** (inline discussion threads)
- **Time Tracking** (timed writing sessions)
- **Focus Mode** (distraction-free writing)

# Core Values

Scenarly is built on three core values that set it apart. With careful analysis of strengths and weaknesses from current market solutions, we crafted a platform that combines the best of all worlds—delivering a complete and seamless writing experience.

While its only 'flaw' is being the new kid on the block, its ambition is clear: to become the **essential**, yet **affordable** alternative for **amateur & professional** screenwriters **who refuse to compromise**.

We are also deeply committed to keeping Scenarly accessible. While infrastructure has a cost, we believe creators shouldn't have to bear that burden alone. We are currently exploring sustainable business models to ensure you never have to pay just to access essential features.

### Transparency

Nothing to hide. In an effort of building trust, Scenarly is source-available as opposed to most well-established screenwriting software

### Modernity

Cloud or offline. Browser or Desktop. Experience a fluid interface, customizable themes, and also real-time collaboration without friction

### Simplicity

Designed from the ground up to free your mind with intuitive workflow. We focus on getting the best out of existing screenwriting tools

# Roadmap

Your feedback is very welcome and ensures Scenarly remains driven by screenwriters—in all their diversity. These ideas are either considered for integration or already in progress. The order does not reflect priority. It is a high priority to make Scenarly feature-rich, without sacrificing its simplicity or stability.

- **Support more formats** (Celtx, FadeIn, docx...)
- **Production reports** (character outline exports, casting...)
- **Mobile app**
- **More statistics**
